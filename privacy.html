Privacy Policy

Brkat's Nexus · last updated 15 November 2026

Brkat's Nexus ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos of the owner's own music to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok.

What data the app uses

When the owner connects a TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and account identifier) through TikTok's Login Kit, limited to the scopes the owner approves (user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish). It uses these only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to confirm which account is connected.

Where it is stored

Tokens and records of what was posted are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other people's data

The app does not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time from TikTok's settings (Security and permissions, then Apps and services), and delete the stored tokens from their computer.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
